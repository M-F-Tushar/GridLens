# How scenario comparisons are computed

`POST /api/scenarios/compare` accepts two full `ScenarioRequest`s (a `base`
and a `candidate`), runs each through the same deterministic
`run_scenario()` used by `/api/scenarios/run`, and then calls
`engine.compare.compare_scenarios(base_result, candidate_result)`.

## What is compared

`kpi_deltas` contains `candidate - base` for every field in
`Totals` (demand, solar generation, grid import/export, curtailment, cost,
emissions, self-consumption ratio, renewable fraction). A negative cost
delta means the candidate is cheaper; a negative emissions delta means the
candidate emits less.

## The narrative layer

`narrative_points` are short, plain-English bullet points generated by
simple, deterministic string templates over the already-computed deltas
(see `engine/compare.py::_build_narrative`). **No LLM is involved in
producing these bullets or the deltas behind them.** A future, LLM-backed
explanation feature may take these exact bullets as *input* and elaborate
on them in more natural prose, with citations — but it may never recompute
or override a number that appears here.

## Requirements for a valid comparison

Both scenarios must use the same `site` to be meaningful (comparing two
different sites' demand profiles is a different kind of question);
GridLens does not currently enforce this, so pay attention to what you are
actually comparing.
